Fleet Party privacy policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026
Publisher: Autosoft (Google Play developer name: autosoftshujaa)
Contact: admin@autosoftshujaa.com

Fleet Party: Pirate Battles is a local multiplayer game for Android TV. Phones join the TV through their web browsers on the same local network. No game account, cloud service, advertising or analytics SDK is included.

Information used for local play

Players choose a captain nickname and one of the supplied portraits. The game creates a random reconnect token and uses fleet placements, mines, targets, shots, scores and match timing to run the game. Captain nicknames, chosen portraits, public battle events and results are shown on the TV and on other participating phones. Private fleet placements remain visible only to their owner until revealed by the game rules. Use a nickname rather than a real name, and do not enter sensitive information.

Phone-to-TV communication uses HTTP and WebSockets over the local network; it is not encrypted. Use a trusted home network. The TV's local IP address is displayed to let phones join. Network addresses are used by the device operating systems to deliver local connections; the app does not build location profiles or retain network-address logs.

Storage and retention

The TV holds the room and player information in memory. Closing/restarting the TV app clears the session. Starting a new party clears the previous room's players and match information. A phone browser stores its reconnect token and setup draft in local browser storage for that TV/room so refreshing can restore the seat. Clear that browser's site data to remove these local values. There is no developer-held account or cloud database to delete.

Transfers and third parties

Gameplay information travels between the participating phones and the TV, and public match information is visible to those in the room. Autosoft does not receive, sell or upload gameplay or captain information. There are no ads, purchases, tracking cookies, remote fonts, analytics, online chat, or integrated Google Play Games sign-in in this version. Google Play and your device/browser providers may separately process information under their own policies when you install or use their products.

Children and families

Fleet Party is designed for family and adult game nights, including players aged 9 and over. There is no public matchmaking or communication with strangers. An adult should supervise the local room and network where children play. Children should use nicknames and should not provide personal information. We do not knowingly receive children's information on a developer-controlled service.

Choices

You can leave a room by closing the phone page, clear browser site data, or ask the TV host to start a new party or close the app. Sound can be muted on the TV. For questions about privacy, contact admin@autosoftshujaa.com.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data handling changes and show the updated effective date.
